Ⅰ

校园学习与 AI 工具

命题预测 6月考了"科研参与"和"校园机器人"，12月很可能延伸至"AI辅助学习""线上与线下学习平衡"等话题。

📝 题目

Directions: For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write a short essay on the topic "The Role of AI Tools in College Learning". You should state your views and give specific suggestions. You should write at least 120 words but no more than 180 words.

✍️ 参考范文

With the rapid development of technology, AI tools like ChatGPT have become increasingly popular among college students. In my view, these tools are a double-edged sword that requires proper guidance.

On the one hand, AI tools bring great convenience to our studies. They can help us quickly find information, explain complex concepts, and even assist in drafting papers. For example, when I encounter a difficult math problem, AI can provide step-by-step solutions, which saves me a lot of time.

On the other hand, over-reliance on AI may weaken our independent thinking ability. Some students simply copy AI-generated answers without understanding the content, which defeats the purpose of learning.

Therefore, I suggest we use AI tools as assistants rather than substitutes for our own thinking. We should first try to solve problems by ourselves and only turn to AI for reference or verification.

In conclusion, AI tools are valuable resources, but the key to true learning still lies in our active engagement and critical thinking.

Ⅱ

青年品德 — 坚持与勇气

命题预测 个人素养类主题会穿插考查，侧重引导考生结合生活实例表达看法。

📝 题目

Directions: For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write a short essay entitled "The Courage of Never Giving Up" by commenting on the saying: "It doesn't take guts to quit. Anyone can quit. And most people do—on their dreams and on themselves." You should write at least 120 words but no more than 180 words.

✍️ 参考范文

Life is a long journey full of unknown challenges. At times, we may encounter stumbling blocks that test our willpower. At such moments, we need the courage to keep going and never give up.

There is a saying that "It doesn't take guts to quit. Anyone can quit. And most people do—on their dreams and on themselves." Indeed, when faced with difficulties, it is always easier to give up than to hold on.

However, constant effort yields certain success. If we pursue our goals with the courage of never giving up, we will eventually succeed in whatever endeavor we take on. Life is just like farming—what you reap depends largely on your sowing and dedication.

Therefore, keep moving forward, even in the toughest times. Never give up and take courage. This courage ultimately determines whether we are winners or losers in the journey of life.

Ⅲ

校园现象 — 考试制度反思

命题预测 6月考了"校园生活便利建议"，12月可能延伸到"大学生心理健康""学业压力"等日常议题。

📝 题目

Directions: For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write a composition entitled "My Views on Examination". You should write at least 120 words according to the outline given below in Chinese:

  1. 大学都用考试来衡量学生的成绩
  2. 考试可能带来的副作用
  3. 我对考试的看法

✍️ 参考范文

Nowadays, examination is used as a chief means of deciding whether a student succeeds or fails in mastering a particular subject in most colleges and universities. Although it is efficient, its side effects are also enormous.

On the one hand, examinations lower the standards of teaching. Since teachers are often judged by examination results, they are reduced to training their students in exam techniques rather than cultivating genuine understanding.

On the other hand, the most undesirable effect is that examinations encourage bad study habits. As the examination score is the only criterion for academic performance, students are driven to memorize mechanically rather than to think creatively.

In fact, few of us would admit that examinations can contribute anything really important to students' academic development. If that is the case, why cannot we make a change and devise something more efficient and reliable than examinations?

I believe a combination of continuous assessment and creative projects would be a better alternative.

💡 备考要点

根据6月真题释放的信号，12月备考需注意：

  • 审题要圈出限定词：6月真题直接给出了"必要性+实用性"等必须回应的维度，漏掉即失分。
  • 具体建议比空话重要："With the development of society"这类套话已难拿高分，应积累"增加设施""优化流程""提供资源"等动作导向的表达。
  • 按话题分类准备素材：教育学习类占比较高，建议每个主题准备3-5个具体案例或表达。
