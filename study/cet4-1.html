SIMULATED QUESTION

The Impact of AI on College Students' Learning

Reference Essay
范文

As a college student who frequently uses AI tools for research, I have witnessed how artificial intelligence is reshaping our learning landscape. While some worry that AI might weaken independent thinking, I believe it serves as a powerful catalyst for efficiency when used responsibly.

Admittedly, AI excels at processing vast amounts of information. It can summarize lengthy papers and generate outlines in seconds, freeing us from repetitive tasks. However, the core of learning lies in critical analysis and creative synthesis, which are uniquely human capabilities. If we rely on AI to do our thinking, we risk becoming passive recipients of information rather than active learners.

Therefore, the key is to treat AI as a collaborative partner, not a replacement. We should use it to brainstorm ideas or check grammar, but the final arguments and insights must be our own. Only by maintaining our intellectual autonomy can we truly benefit from this technological revolution.

亮点解析
  • 去模板化开头： 用"As a college student who..."直接切入个人视角，替代了空洞的"Nowadays..."。
  • 思辨性论证： 没有简单地说"有利有弊"，而是具体指出了AI的优势（处理信息）和人类的不可替代性（批判性思维）。
  • 具体建议： 结尾给出了"collaborative partner"的具体定位，而非泛泛而谈的"we should use it properly"。

SIMULATED QUESTION

How to Manage Time Effectively in College

Reference Essay
范文

College life offers unprecedented freedom, but this freedom often leads to procrastination and anxiety for many students. Effective time management is not just about squeezing more hours into a day; it is about prioritizing what truly matters.

The first step is to distinguish between "urgent" and "important" tasks. Many students spend hours on trivial assignments while neglecting long-term goals like skill acquisition or career planning. Using digital planners or the Pomodoro technique can help visualize these priorities and maintain focus. More importantly, we must learn to say "no" to distractions. In an era of constant notifications, protecting our attention is a form of self-discipline.

Ultimately, managing time is managing ourselves. By establishing a balanced routine that includes study, rest, and social interaction, we can navigate college life with both purpose and peace of mind.

亮点解析
  • 逻辑递进： 按照"提出问题（自由导致拖延）- 分析原因/方法（区分轻重缓急）- 升华主题（管理时间即管理自我）"的逻辑展开。
  • 具体案例： 提到了"Pomodoro technique（番茄工作法）"和"digital planners"，比单纯说"make a plan"更有说服力。
  • 地道表达： 使用了"procrastination"、"trivial assignments"、"self-discipline"等精准词汇。

The Importance of Interdisciplinary Learning

Reference Essay
范文

In today's rapidly evolving world, complex problems rarely fit into a single academic discipline. This is why interdisciplinary learning has become indispensable for college students. It breaks down the walls between majors and fosters innovative thinking.

Take environmental protection as an example. Solving climate change requires not only biological knowledge but also economic policies and ethical considerations. When students from different backgrounds collaborate, they bring unique perspectives that lead to more comprehensive solutions. Furthermore, interdisciplinary exposure prepares us for the real job market, where employers value adaptability and diverse skill sets over narrow expertise.

To embrace this trend, universities should offer more cross-departmental courses, and students should step out of their comfort zones to explore unfamiliar fields. After all, true innovation often happens at the intersection of different disciplines.

亮点解析
  • 具体论证： 用"环境保护"作为具体案例，解释了为什么需要跨学科（生物+经济+伦理），避免了空洞的说理。
  • 紧扣现实： 提到了"real job market"和"employers value"，将学习与未来职业发展挂钩，体现了现实意义。
  • 行动导向： 结尾分别给学校和学生提出了具体建议，符合6月真题强调的"提出具体方案"的趋势。

📝 12月备考核心策略（基于最新评分标准）

根据6月真题释放的信号，12月备考需注意：

1
严禁堆砌模板
2026年评分标准明确规定，模板化语句超过30%直接降档。请放弃"Every coin has two sides"这类万能句，改用逻辑连接词（如 Admittedly, However, Consequently）来衔接观点。
2
内容质量 > 语言华丽
阅卷老师现在更看重"观点是否有深度"和"案例是否具体"。哪怕语法简单一点，只要逻辑清晰、言之有物，分数也会比空洞的华丽文章高。
3
审题圈画限定词
12月题目可能会像6月一样给出限定维度（如"必要性+实用性"）。动笔前务必圈出题目要求回应的所有点，确保不遗漏。
4
积累"动作导向"表达
针对建议类题目，多积累 implement, optimize, allocate, prioritize 等动词，让文章看起来更有执行力。
